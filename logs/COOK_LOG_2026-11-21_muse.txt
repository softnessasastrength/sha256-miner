THE FINDING — POOL DIAGNOSTIC PROBE (appended to the LIVE_RUN log)
At ~11M hashes with no share (expected ~4.29M/share at difficulty
0.001; P(no share) ~ 0.08), a brief independent stratum probe
(subscribe + authorize only, then disconnect; the hunt was not
disturbed) captured the pool's actual assignment verbatim:
  subscribe -> result [[['mining.notify','d88fad7f']], 'd88fad7f', 8]
  authorize -> result true
  mining.set_difficulty -> [100000]
  mining.notify -> real job; coinbase contains "Public-Pool".
So: egress OPEN, pool REACHED, worker AUTHORISED, real work FLOWING —
but public-pool.io assigns this worker difficulty 100,000, not the
0.001 minimum yesterday's web research reported. At the measured
~23,600 H/s the expected time to one share at difficulty 100,000 is
2^32 x 100000 / 23600 ~ 1.8e10 s ~ 577 years (arithmetic from measured
numbers). The hunt is therefore not viable at this pool at bridge
hashrate, regardless of patience. The running client's own session is
consistent with this (13M+ hashes, no share).
