=== DESIGN ===
rtl/sha256_pipe.v — sha256_pipe: 64 stages of sha256_pipe_stage, one
round per stage, one block accepted per clock once full. Per-stage
state: vars (256) + chaining value (256) + schedule window (512) +
unconsumed block words (512, stages 0..15 only — WITH_BLK parameter).
Round math identical to rtl/sha256_core.v. Registered final add;
latency 65 clocks; results in order, one per clock.
rtl/sha256d_pipe_search.v — three sha256_pipe cores cascaded
(A: header block 1; B: header block 2 + nonce; C: hash of digest),
nonce carried as a tag through 65-deep shift registers per hop.
Throughput 1 hash/clock at full rate; first hit = lowest nonce.
Same external interface as sha256d_search.

DERIVED (SYNTHESIS ESTIMATE, not a benchmark): one pipe at the
routed Fmax produces 1 hash/clock = ~50.15 MH/s — about 190x the
iterative unit's 0.26 MH/s estimate. One pipe is what fits: the
full 3-pipe mining cascade needs 3 x 64,481 = 193,443 pipeline FF
alone against the device's 83,640 total, and even a 2-pipe
midstate variant (128,962 FF) exceeds it. The pipelined MINER
therefore does not fit the LFE5U-85F; its homes are a larger FPGA
or the sky130 ASIC route (where the same arithmetic applies in
area: 3 pipes ~ 3x the single pipe's cell area). The single pipe's
fit at 77% FF is itself the useful SPOON-1 datapoint: pipeline
registers, not logic, are the currency.
